---
title: nPanel
description: Host nsites as normal websites on your own domain.
order: 3
---

nPanel runs nsites as real websites: connect a domain you own, publish the site to Nostr from any client — republish the nsite manifest event from anywhere — and it's served to visitors. Changes are picked up automatically, or refresh on demand. Uses the [nPanel gateway service](/services/npanel).

Every site you host can also carry **NIP-05 names**: assign `name@yourdomain` identities to any npub, right next to the site it belongs to. So `example.com` serves your nsite and `sam@example.com` resolves to your Nostr key — one domain, both jobs.
