---
title: nPanel
description: Host nsites as normal websites on your own domain.
order: 3
---

nPanel runs nsites as real websites: connect a domain you own, publish the site to Nostr from any client, and it's served to visitors — with NIP-05 names on the same host. Changes are picked up automatically, or refresh on demand. Uses the [nPanel gateway service](/services/npanel).
